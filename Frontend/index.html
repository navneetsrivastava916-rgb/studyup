Study Tips
⏰
Increase Study Time
2-hour blocks + 10 min breaks (Pomodoro). Sleep 10:30 PM, wake 5 AM. Remove phone from room. Build: 4hrs → 6hrs → 8hrs → 10hrs/day.
✅
Complete Every Task
Do hardest task first (eat the frog). Break big chapters into daily goals. Track every hour. 30 min is better than 0.
🚧
Remove Barriers
Delete social media apps or use grayscale mode. Track daily wins. Feeling behind? Focus only on today, not the total gap.
📊
Smart Strategy
Physics: HC Verma + DC Pandey. Chemistry: NCERT for Inorganic. Maths: Cengage. Do 30 MCQs per subject daily minimum.
🧠
Spaced Revision
Revise within 24hrs, 1 week, 1 month. Short notes + formula flashcards. Never study new topics 2 weeks before exam.
