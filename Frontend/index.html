Study Tips
⏰
Increase Study Time
2-hour blocks + 10-min breaks (Pomodoro). Sleep by 11 PM, wake at 5 AM. Remove phone from study room. Start small: 4hrs → 6hrs → 8hrs → 10hrs.
✅
Complete Every Task
Break big chapters into small daily goals. Do hardest task first (eat the frog). Track every hour. Never skip a day — 30 min is better than 0.
🚧
Remove Barriers
Social media: delete apps or use grayscale. Low motivation: track daily wins. Confusion: strictly follow schedule. Feeling behind: focus only on today.
📊
Smart Study Strategy
Physics: HC Verma + DC Pandey + PYQs. Chemistry: NCERT for Inorganic, MS Chauhan for Organic. Maths: Cengage + PYQs. 30 MCQs per subject daily minimum.
🧠
Revision Formula
Revise within 24 hrs, 1 week, 1 month (spaced repetition). Short notes + flashcards for formulas. Never study new topics 2 weeks before exam.
